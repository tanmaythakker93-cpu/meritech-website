What's included

The full
cloud journey.

Cloud transformation
Strategy and roadmap to move your organisation to the cloud — aligning the transformation with business objectives, not just relocating servers. We define the cloud target state and the phased path to get there, de-risking migration and ensuring the move adds measurable value.
Migration & integration
Data & apps migration from on-premise or between clouds, integrated with the systems that remain — without breaking what works. Meritech handles sequencing, rollback planning, and the integrations that keep hybrid environments functional during and after the move.
Cloud-native development
Building applications designed for the cloud from the ground up — elastic, resilient, and built to scale with demand rather than ahead of it. Microservices, serverless, and event-driven architectures that take full advantage of what the cloud actually offers.
Orchestration — Kubernetes, Docker & SWARM
Container orchestration with Kubernetes, Docker, and SWARM so workloads deploy, scale, and recover automatically. Self-healing infrastructure that adjusts to demand without manual intervention — the operational foundation for any serious cloud-native estate.
Multi-cloud across AWS, Azure & GCP
Deep expertise across AWS, Azure, and GCP — selecting and combining providers to fit your workloads, compliance needs, and commercial requirements. Provider lock-in should be a deliberate choice, not the default outcome of a single-vendor engagement.

Questions to ask

Before you choose
a cloud partner.

Lift-and-shift, or true modernization?
A clear partner tells you which approach the workload actually needs — and is honest when lift-and-shift is sufficient versus when re-architecting captures more value. Meritech assesses each workload independently and builds the roadmap around the answer, not the invoice.
Can you work across AWS, Azure, and GCP?
Provider lock-in should be a deliberate choice, not a default outcome. Meritech is provider-agnostic — we have teams certified across AWS, Azure, and GCP, and we choose and combine providers based on your workloads and commercial needs, not reseller incentives.
How do you handle integration with what stays?
Most migrations are hybrid for a while — integration with what remains on-premise or in another cloud matters as much as the migration itself. Meritech designs the integration layer upfront, not as an afterthought, so there are no broken connections when workloads move.
What's your orchestration approach?
Kubernetes, Docker, and SWARM are what make scale and recovery automatic. Without proper orchestration, cloud workloads still require manual scaling and intervention. Meritech builds orchestration into the architecture from day one, not bolted on later.
How do you keep costs under control after migration?
Meritech's zoECON™ Cloud Cost Optimizer — powered by the C2O AI Engine — delivers up to 60% reduction in cloud spend through AI-driven cost recommendations and security validation. FinOps is built into the engagement, not sold separately after the bill grows.
