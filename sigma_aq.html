Sigma-AQ

The test that
runs itself

Sigma-AQ is a smartphone-based network and service testing solution for site verification, troubleshooting, optimization and benchmarking. Run scripted tests indoors and outdoors with live radio and signalling views, then hand the logs to a connected upload and reporting workflow.

Overview

Engineered for field autonomy. Built for ground-truth accuracy.

Sigma-AQ is designed for the engineer on the move. Running on supported commercial Android smartphones, it measures service quality on the kind of device your subscribers carry.

When the full workflow is configured, logs move from the phone to upload, processing and template reporting with far less repetitive handling. Execute walk tests, drive tests, SSV, optimization and competitive benchmarking from one application. Supported handsets, tests and quality algorithms depend on the device, firmware and licence package.

Scripted
Scripted Tests

Voice, data, streaming, OTT, HTTP/FTP, iPerf, ping, traceroute and packet-capture tests run from a script.

Highlights

What makes
Sigma-AQ different.

Runs on end-user devices

Supported on Samsung Galaxy S25 series, OnePlus 15, and Xiaomi 15. Testing on current commercial devices gives a representative view of what subscribers on your network experience. Supported handsets depend on chipset, firmware and licence package.

Connected reporting

Logs upload through the configured workflow, so KPI processing and template reports can follow without manual file handling.

One tool, many scenarios

SSV, troubleshooting, optimization, competitive benchmarking, indoor, outdoor, drive and walk tests, all from one application.

Indoor and outdoor

Shopping malls, offices, subways, boats, airports, and event venues — Sigma-AQ measures networks from a subscriber perspective, wherever subscribers actually are.

Why it matters.

Sigma-AQ folds measurement, application testing, and indoor coverage into a single Android-based tool, with remote control through Sigma-AQ Boss. The tabs below show what it measures, what it runs, and why it matters.

  • Scripted service testing on the smartphone
  • Mapping of results in the field
  • Saves logs for replay and upload
  • Live radio and signaling views, with network events
  • Packet capture, run as a configured operation
  • Replays saved logs after the session
  • Runs on Samsung Galaxy S25, OnePlus 15, Xiaomi 15, Sony Xperia
  • Multi-RAT: 5G NR, LTE, WCDMA, GSM
  • GPS outdoors and location marking indoors
  • Multiple devices in a coordinated session with Sigma-AQ Boss
  • Voice, data, streaming, OTT, HTTP/FTP, iPerf, ping, traceroute and packet capture tests
  • Remote control via Sigma-AQ Boss from the office
