Sigma-AQ

The test that
runs itself

Sigma-AQ is a smartphone-based network and service testing solution for site verification, troubleshooting, optimisation and benchmarking. Run scripted tests indoors and outdoors with live radio and signalling views, then hand the logs to a connected upload and reporting workflow.

Overview

Engineered for field autonomy. Built for ground-truth accuracy.

Sigma-AQ is designed for the engineer on the move. Running on supported commercial Android smartphones, it measures service quality on the kind of device your subscribers carry. When the full workflow is configured, logs move from the phone to upload, processing and template reporting with far less repetitive handling. Execute walk tests, drive tests, SSV, optimisation and competitive benchmarking from one application. Supported handsets, tests and quality algorithms depend on the device, firmware and licence package.

Scripted Tests

Why it matters.

Sigma-AQ folds measurement, application testing, and indoor coverage into a single Android-based tool, with remote control through Sigma-AQ Boss. The tabs below show what it measures, what it runs, and why it matters.

  • Scripted service testing on the smartphone
  • Mapping of results in the field
  • Saves logs for replay and upload
  • Live radio and signalling views, with network events
  • Packet capture, run as a configured operation
  • Replays saved logs after the session
  • Runs on supported commercial Android smartphones; contact us for current device, firmware and licence compatibility.
  • 5G NR, LTE and supported legacy technologies, depending on device and licence
  • GPS outdoors and location marking indoors
  • Multiple devices in a coordinated session with Sigma-AQ Boss
  • Voice, data, streaming, OTT, HTTP/FTP, iPerf, ping, traceroute and packet capture tests
  • Remote test control through the configured Sigma-AQ Boss service
  • Tests on the kind of commercial device subscribers use
  • Remote control and log upload reduce repeated manual steps
  • One application for supported drive, walk, verification and benchmarking scenarios
  • Shared scripts and methods help standardise field execution
  • Template reports produced through the configured server workflow
  • Requires a supported Android device and build; diagnostic access, tokens or device preparation may be needed for particular functions.
