Questions to ask

Before you choose
a managed IT partner.

Is monitoring proactive or reactive?
Catching issues early is the entire value of managed IT. Meritech monitors applications and infrastructure proactively — issues are caught as they emerge and handled before they become outages, rather than ticketed after your users have already felt them.
Do you optimize, or just keep the lights on?
Uptime is the floor; optimization is the value. Meritech continuously optimizes performance and resource use across the estate — so systems don't just stay running, they stay efficient as workloads and demands change.
How is cloud cost managed, not just uptime?
A healthy cloud with a runaway bill is not a managed cloud. Meritech manages cloud environments for performance and cost-efficiency together — the estate stays healthy and the spend stays watched, quarter after quarter.
What does stringent security mean in practice?
Ask for the ongoing controls, not a one-time audit. Meritech maintains security continuously across the managed estate — controls, vigilance, and response that operate every day, not an assessment that ages the moment it's filed.
How do you extend rather than replace our team?
The best managed IT augments your people. Meritech runs the estate so your team doesn't have to — your people keep the context and the strategic work, while the monitoring, operations, and security run as a managed service beneath them.
