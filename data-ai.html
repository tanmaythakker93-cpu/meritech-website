Questions to ask

Before you choose
a data partner.

Can you integrate our scattered data sources?
Insight is only as good as the data behind it — integration comes first. Meritech designs the integration layer upfront, not after the fact, so scattered data across databases, APIs, files, and IoT streams is brought into one managed estate before analysis begins.
Do dashboards update in real time?
A stale dashboard is just a report. Meritech's Power BI solutions are designed to be live — interactive visualizations that update as the business moves, so decisions are grounded in the current state of operations, not last week's export.
Can you move us from reporting to predicting?
The value step-change in data maturity is moving from explaining the past to forecasting what comes next. Meritech's predictive analysis capability builds on your integrated data estate and turns historical patterns into forward-looking models that shape strategy — not just reports.
Where does AI genuinely add value for us?
A good partner scopes AI to real outcomes, not hype. Meritech assesses each engagement independently — identifying where AI automation, classification, or prediction genuinely adds value versus where simpler analysis is sufficient. The goal is measurable impact, not a technology showcase.
Do you handle big data at scale?
Volume changes the architecture — and Meritech designs for big data from the start. Integration and migration at scale, across high-volume, high-velocity data sources, requires different tooling and architecture than standard BI. Ask whether a partner has built for scale before — not whether they can adapt later.
