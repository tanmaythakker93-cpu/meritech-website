Questions to ask

Before you choose a
development partner.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?
A new product that can't talk to your current stack creates another silo. Meritech's integration architects design specifically to connect — every new build is assessed against your existing IT landscape, and integration via APIs and protocols is built in, not bolted on.
Do you cover the full lifecycle?
Ideation to deployment with one team avoids costly hand-off gaps. Meritech delivers end-to-end — from the first workflow design and UX blueprint through to a deployed, supported production system — with one accountable team across every stage.
How do you handle UX and security?
High-UX and secure shouldn't be a trade-off. Meritech builds both in from the start — UX is designed before code is written, and security is treated as an architectural requirement, not a compliance checkbox. Ask any vendor how both are designed in, not added later.
Web, mobile, hybrid, or PWA — which and why?
The right platform choice should be reasoned from your users and business goals, not the vendor's existing habits. Meritech works across web, native mobile, hybrid, and PWA — the recommendation comes from what fits the user's context and your operational requirements, not from what we build most often.
What's your track record and longevity?
20+ years of evolving web and mobile experience is a different risk profile than a newer shop. Meritech has been building digital products through multiple cycles of platform change — from early web to mobile-first to cloud-native — and the cumulative experience reduces risk on every new engagement.
Will the solution align with our business objectives?
Technology should serve outcomes — not run parallel to them. Every Meritech Digital Synergy engagement starts with the business objective, not the technical approach. Ask any vendor how the build maps to your goals — and whether they'll measure success the same way you do.
