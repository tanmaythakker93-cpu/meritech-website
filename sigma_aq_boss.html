Overview

One controller for a group of test devices.

Coordinate a group of probes with fewer repeated manual actions. Sigma-AQ Boss supports the control arrangement selected for the deployment, with defined probe capacity, connectivity and integration into the upload and reporting workflow.

The Sigma suite ecosystem

Field Collection
Sigma-AQ

The smartphone test probe that Sigma-AQ Boss coordinates. Network and service testing for site verification, optimisation and benchmarking.
Central Management
Centra-SD

Central management of test devices and measurement data, with uploaded logs, map-based KPI views and configured reporting.
Post-Processing
Sigma-PA

Windows post-processing and analysis for detailed investigation: log replay, L3 and PCAP analysis, KPI calculation and Excel reports.
Drive Test Platform
Sigma-LA

Windows drive test for up to 4 UEs, 2 scan receivers and a GPS. Works alongside Sigma-AQ probes when scanner data is needed.
