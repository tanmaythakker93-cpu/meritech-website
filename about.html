Who we are

Built for one
connected system.

Every connected experience rests on infrastructure that has to be measured, powered, and run. Networks must prove their performance. Devices must run for years on almost no power. Enterprise systems must deliver more while costing less.

Meritech works on all three. From Tokyo and offices across seven countries, our software and RF engineers — specialists in NR5G, LTE, and WCDMA — build the test systems, IoT platforms, and IT services that make connected technology efficient. We work closely with global network operators and vendors, customizing solutions to industry standards and to each client's requirements.

We have done this for more than twenty years, guided by one principle: your success is our success. Your efficiency, our passion.

Our journey

Two decades of
building forward.

From wireless test & measurement to healthcare connectivity, IoT, and edge AI — each step expanded what Meritech could do for its customers.

2010
The Sigma Suite

Meritech expanded its product offerings by introducing the Sigma Suite — a range of wireless solutions designed to enhance Test & Measurement products for RF optimization.

2016
Into healthcare, with Mesimo

A pivotal move into medical healthcare — launching Mesimo, a private MVNO, positioning Meritech as a player in healthcare technology and cellular IoT connectivity.

2019
monoZ enters the IoT portfolio

Meritech unveiled monoZ, an addition to its IoT portfolio that became the foundation of the monoZ ecosystem.

2020
Acquiring Eoxys

A significant acquisition — integrating Eoxys, an AI/ML-based IoT company, into the Meritech ecosystem, bringing edge AI capability (now the Xeno+ suite) in-house.

2025
The 1NCE collaboration

monoZ and 1NCE joined forces to simplify global IoT deployments — as a certified integration partner, integrating 1NCE's global IoT platform supporting 30 million connected products across 173 countries.
