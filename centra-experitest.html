Overview

A tool built to measure what subscribers measure you by.

KPIs tell you the network is working. Centra-Experitest tells you whether the apps subscribers use every day are actually working — on a real smartphone, in real conditions, tested the same way a real user would encounter them.

Working with Digital.ai's Seetest Cloud, it automates and monitors all Mobile and WebApp test items on your smartphone on a regular basis, 24×7. When a service anomaly is detected, it notifies the team with an alarm. Users can notice anomalies before customer complaints — and act on them before the experience degrades.

The pipeline

Three pillars.
One truth.

Centra-Experitest addresses the three things that matter to the subscriber: what it costs to monitor them, whether the experience is real, and whether you can see the problem before it surfaces.

01 / Cost Reduction
Manual confirmation, automated away.

In the past, the confirmation scan was done manually. Measurement results are now automatically collected and judged. The number of monitoring items and monitoring frequency can be easily increased — which significantly reduces the cost of network monitoring.

02 / Quality of Experience
Real smartphone, real user environment.

The quality of experience is measured on a real smartphone as the real user environment. A variety of Mobile Apps and Web Apps are compatible. Monitoring many items frequently contributes directly to QoE improvement.

03 / Performance Visualization
Network performance, made visible.

Monitor and visualize network performance using the speed test app on every subscriber's phone. Download and install apps and monitor network performance using video sites — the same way a subscriber would.
