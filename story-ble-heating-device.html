BLE Heating Device

A next-generation heating device with Bluetooth Low Energy connectivity — compact hardware built around a 250mAh Li-Ion rechargeable battery on a PCB just 12mm × 30mm, with embedded software for wireless connectivity and a cloud management system for device oversight and data collection. Meritech engineered the whole chain, from copper traces to dashboard.

Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

A heater the size of a fingertip, managed from the cloud.

The initiative was to develop a next-generation heating device with Bluetooth Low Energy connectivity. That meant three engineering problems in one: compact hardware powered by a 250mAh Li-Ion rechargeable battery, embedded software for the wireless connectivity, and a cloud management system for device oversight and data collection — because a connected device without the system behind it is just a device.

Approach & Solution

From a 12 × 30 millimetre board to a full cloud backend.

01
Hardware engineered on a 12mm × 30mm PCB

The entire device — battery management, heating control, and BLE radio — was engineered onto a printed circuit board measuring just 12 by 30 millimetres, small enough to disappear into the product it powers.

02
Complete embedded hardware architecture

The full embedded hardware architecture was designed in-house using OrCAD Capture and Routing — schematic to layout under one roof, so the size, power, and thermal constraints were solved together rather than negotiated between vendors.

03
Embedded software for wireless connectivity

Device firmware written in Embedded C on FreeRTOS handles the BLE connectivity, battery-efficient operation, and heating control — real-time software sized for a small, battery-powered device.

04
Backend cloud dashboard, built to specification

A cloud dashboard developed to the client's specifications gives them oversight of the device fleet — remote management, control, and data collection from every unit in the field.

Business Value

Their design. Their data. Their competitive edge.

Proprietary design owned by the customer — the hardware and its IP belong to the client outright, not licensed from a module vendor.
Efficient battery operation — the device runs on a 250mAh Li-Ion rechargeable battery, with firmware tuned to make a small cell last.
Remotely managed and controlled — every device in the field is visible and controllable through the cloud dashboard application.
Data collection built in — the cloud system gathers usage and device data continuously, giving the client a picture of their product in the real world.
