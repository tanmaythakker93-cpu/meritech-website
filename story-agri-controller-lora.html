Agri Controller Gateway with LoRa

A major Indian multinational wanted to modernize its water irrigation control system — replacing an expensive, bulky setup with a custom PLC controller with LoRa connectivity. Meritech engineered the device end to end on its own XENO+ LoRa ML SOM: compact, cost-effective, and wired for 24 channels of sensors and valves.

Objective

One compact controller. Twenty-four channels. LoRa to the field.

The goal was to build an agri water irrigation controller device with LoRa connectivity — a purpose-built replacement for the client's existing bulky, high-cost setup. The controller had to speak to the field directly, with a specific interface set: 5× 4-20mA current sensor inputs, 5× digital inputs, 2× 0-10V analog inputs, and 12× valve control relays — everything an irrigation site needs to sense and act, in one device.

Approach & Solution

Built on our own silicon carrier: the XENO+ SOM.

01
Embedded hardware designed on the XENO+ LoRa ML SOM

The device's embedded hardware was designed around Meritech's own XENO+ LoRa ML system-on-module — LoRa connectivity and edge intelligence already proven on the module, so the custom engineering effort went into the irrigation-specific I/O, not reinventing the radio.

02
Customized embedded software, tuned to the project

The device's embedded software was developed in-house and tuned to the project's exact requirements — reading five current-loop sensors, five digital and two analog inputs, and driving twelve valve relays reliably in the field.

Business Value

Smaller box. Smaller bill. Same fields, better control.

Bulky, high-cost system replaced — the customer's existing setup gave way to a compact and cost-effective custom controller, purpose-built for the job.
Software tuned to the project's requirements — no paying for, powering, or maintaining features the irrigation system doesn't need.
LoRa connectivity built in — long-range, low-power communication suited to fields and pump houses far from wired infrastructure.
Proven SOM at the core — building on the XENO+ LoRa ML module shortened the path from requirement to working device.
