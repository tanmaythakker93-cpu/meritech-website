The Meritech approach

Connectivity chosen for
the use case, not the vendor.

Most IoT connectivity failures are decisions made too early — a protocol picked before the duty cycle was known, a network deployed before the coverage map was checked, integration bolted onto infrastructure that was never designed for IoT. Meritech Connectivity Solutions works the other way: understand the device, the data volume, the geography, and the power budget first — then select and deploy the connectivity layer that fits.

Whether that is LPWA, cellular, hybrid, or a combination across a multi-geography fleet, the answer comes from your requirements, not a product catalogue — backed by twenty-five years of wireless and IoT field experience.

What's included

Every layer of
IoT connectivity.

Communication protocol selection
Selecting the right IoT messaging protocol for your specific use case — MQTT for lightweight pub-sub over constrained networks, CoAP for request-response over UDP, LWM2M for device management integration, or AMQP for enterprise broker architectures — with the choice driven by payload size, latency requirement, QoS level, security posture, and device memory constraints.
Network architecture & technology selection
Designing the network topology and selecting the radio access technology — LPWA (NB-IoT, LTE-M, LoRa), cellular (4G/5G), Wi-Fi, or hybrid — on evidence: coverage in your actual deployment geography, message volume, latency tolerance, battery life expectancy, and cost per endpoint over the fleet's operational lifetime.
Network deployment & rollout management
End-to-end management of the network build — from lab validation and site planning through field deployment and acceptance testing. Meritech coordinates SIM provisioning, gateway placement, network configuration, and acceptance criteria so the deployed network matches the architecture as designed, not just as documented.
IT infrastructure integration
Connecting the IoT connectivity layer to your existing ERP, SCADA, MES, cloud platform, or data warehouse — with a standardised integration architecture that avoids custom glue code per system. IoT data flows into the applications that use it, without rebuilding the infrastructure around each new device type.
Ongoing network management & monitoring
Continuous monitoring of network performance, connectivity uptime, and SIM utilisation across the live fleet — with proactive alerting, capacity planning, and carrier management to keep the network running as the fleet grows, roams across geographies, or shifts to new device generations.

Questions to ask

Before you choose an
IoT connectivity partner.

How do you choose between LPWA and cellular?
The choice is driven by four factors: power budget (how long must the battery last), coverage (does the deployment geography have NB-IoT or LTE-M coverage, or is LoRa needed), data volume (how many bytes per message, how often), and cost per endpoint over the fleet's lifetime. Meritech evaluates all four with your actual numbers before recommending a technology — not on the basis of which network a carrier is pushing.
Which IoT protocol is right for our use case?
Protocol selection depends on your transport layer, device memory, latency requirement, and broker infrastructure. MQTT suits low-bandwidth, intermittent connections over TCP; CoAP fits UDP-based constrained devices; LWM2M adds device management; AMQP suits enterprise broker integration. Meritech maps each against your device profile and infrastructure before making a recommendation.
Can you integrate with our existing SCADA or ERP system?
Yes. Meritech designs a standardised integration architecture that connects IoT data flows to your existing operational and enterprise systems — SCADA, MES, ERP, cloud data warehouses, or custom APIs — using well-defined data models rather than bespoke connectors that become a maintenance liability as the fleet and the enterprise systems evolve.
What does network management include after deployment?
Ongoing management covers connectivity uptime monitoring, SIM utilisation tracking, carrier performance benchmarking, alert management, and capacity planning. As the fleet grows, roams across geographies, or shifts to new device generations, the network management service ensures the connectivity layer keeps pace — without reactive firefighting every time something changes.
How do you handle connectivity across multiple geographies?
Multi-geography fleets require network technology selection per region — coverage, regulation, and carrier maturity differ significantly between markets. Meritech designs a connectivity architecture that accounts for regional variation, combined with Mesimo's global cellular IoT platform where a single SIM and contract covers the fleet across all deployment territories.
