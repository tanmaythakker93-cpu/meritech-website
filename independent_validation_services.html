Process

Questions to ask

Before you choose
a validation partner.

How are development and validation roles handled?
Responsibilities and any independence requirements are defined for the engagement before work starts.
Which testing methods are used?
The delivery team agrees suitable methods, environments and coverage for the system and its requirements.
What can the engagement cover?
The scope can include selected software functions, systems, integrations or delivery processes, as agreed with the delivery team.
How is performance assessed?
Define the workload, environment, measurement method and target criteria before testing.
What does the report include?
The agreed test results, identified issues, supporting evidence and relevant scope limitations.
