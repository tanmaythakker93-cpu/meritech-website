Common Questions

Before you talk to
an engineer.

What does Edge to Cloud (E2C) mean for an IoT project?
Edge to Cloud (E2C) means your IoT architecture spans from the physical sensing layer — hardware, firmware, and LPWA modules on the device — all the way to cloud-side data pipelines, dashboards, and analytics. Meritech products cover every layer, so you can assemble a complete stack from one partner.
What's the difference between the monoZ products?
monoZ products sit at different layers. monoZ:Zero and monoZ:Jet are hardware dev kits for prototyping. monoZ:SDK and monoZ:Connect Service are software libraries. monoZ:Link and monoZ:Connect are cloud-side platforms. They are designed to interoperate — pick what your project needs.
Which connectivity technologies does Meritech support?
Meritech supports LTE-M, NB-IoT, 4G, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, BLE, LoRa, and more across the monoZ and Xeno+ product families. Mesimo adds global MVNO connectivity across 3 cellular technologies in 173 countries through a partnership with 1NCE.
What is Xeno+ and how is it different from monoZ?
Xeno+ is a solderable Edge ML SOM module — it adds audio-processing AI/ML capability to any IoT design without requiring a board redesign. monoZ is an LPWA connectivity suite (hardware + software + cloud). They serve different layers and are complementary, not competing.
Can Meritech support a deployment globally?
Yes. Meritech operates across Japan, India, North America, and Europe with multi-language customer support teams. Mesimo provides cellular IoT connectivity in 173 countries, and Meritech's professional services team can support the full deployment lifecycle globally.
