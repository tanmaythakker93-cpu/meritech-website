Overview

The gap between plan
and performance.

A network that meets its KPIs on commissioning day will drift. Traffic grows, interference patterns change, handover boundaries erode. Optimization is the continuous work of keeping the field aligned with intent — and closing the gap when it isn't.

Meritech's optimization engineers work from drive test data, network counters, and radio parameter analysis to identify what's degrading performance and what needs to change. The starting point is always measurement. The endpoint is always the subscriber.

Measure. Diagnose. Simulate. Consult.

Four disciplines.
One alignment target.

01
Audio quality measurement & evaluation

Field measurement of voice and data quality using drive and walk tests. Objective QoS/QoE KPIs benchmarked against target thresholds — giving the optimization team a ground-truth baseline before changes are recommended.

02
Radio quality problem analysis

Root cause investigation of coverage gaps, interference events, dropped calls, and handover failures. Protocol-level decode alongside RF measurement identifies whether the cause is coverage, interference, configuration, or capacity — before any parameter is touched.

03
Area Optimization

Site-by-site and cluster-level parameter tuning to close coverage gaps, reduce interference, and improve handover success rates. Changes are modelled first, then validated by post-optimization drive test to confirm the improvement against the baseline.

04
Consulting for wireless technology

Strategic advisory on technology evolution, spectrum deployment, and network architecture. Operators navigating 5G NR rollout, LTE refarming, or multi-layer interference challenges engage Meritech as an independent technical partner with no vendor bias.
