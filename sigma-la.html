Sigma-LA

The quiet instrument
behind better networks.

Sigma-LA is the Windows-based drive test platform that captures, monitors and saves RF log data from multiple UEs, scan receivers and GPS devices — simultaneously. The origin point for every network insight, every optimization decision, every acceptance report.

Overview

A tool built for verification, not demonstration.

For two decades, the engineers who optimize the world's mobile networks have trusted one Windows-based drive test platform to capture the ground truth. Sigma-LA doesn't chase trends — it sets the standard.

It verifies radio network quality through drive tests and supports competitive measurement across networks, with data collection for NR5G, LTE, WCDMA, GSM and GPRS — every technology generation still operating in the field today. Out-of-the-box KPIs mean an engineer arrives at site ready to capture, not ready to configure.

Parallel Capture

Simultaneous multi-technology capture across up to four UEs in a single drive session.

Independent RF sweep at greater than 1 GPS fix per second — continuous scan coverage.

Main features

Three pillars.
One purpose.

Sigma-LA integrates monitoring, display and troubleshooting as a single workflow. Designed by engineers who have spent their careers doing drive tests at operator sites.

  • Communicates automatically with attached UEs to collect Layer 1, 2 & 3 data directly from the chipset
  • Live data displayed on maps as the drive happens
  • Saves logged data to disk for further analysis
  • Displays collected data from Layer 1 to Layer 3 in realtime across multiple screens
  • Real-time L3 decoding with full packet decode
  • Imports and replays saved log data after the session
  • Windows-based platform — runs on standard laptops engineers already carry
  • Multi-RAT: NR5G, LTE, WCDMA, GSM and GPRS simultaneous capture
  • Up to 4× UE parallel capture with independent RF sweep receivers
  • ETSI TS 102 250 and ITU-T E.804 QoS/QoE KPI calculation
  • 100+ built-in KPIs: throughput, per-radio, and quality metrics
  • Export to MS Excel, SQL, CSV, KMZ, and standard industry formats
  • No pre-test scripting — arrive at site ready to capture, not ready to configure
  • Two decades of operator field deployment across every major RAT
  • Seamlessly feeds Sigma-PA for post-processing without file conversion
  • Full L1–L4 decode depth not available from over-the-air or probe-only tools
  • Same tool for SSV, optimization, vendor acceptance, and benchmarking
  • Log format natively accepted by Sigma-PA and Centra-SD without conversion

The post-processing platform that ingests Sigma-LA logs natively. The file that left the field is the file that gets analysed — no conversion, no data loss.

Android-based drive and walk test alongside Sigma-LA. Continuous monitoring and benchmarking across live networks, on the same platform.
