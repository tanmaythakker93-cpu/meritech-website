Common Questions

Before you talk
to an engineer.

What is Mesimo?
Mesimo is the cellular IoT connectivity service offered by monoZ, in partnership with leading network operators. Rather than negotiating coverage operator-by-operator, Mesimo gives your deployment a single connectivity layer — with the right technology (4G LTE, LTE-M, or NB-IoT) chosen per device, and network management handled by the platform.
Which connectivity technologies are available?
Mesimo supports three technologies: 4G LTE for high-throughput devices like gateways and cameras; LTE-M for mobile assets and wearables that need moderate bandwidth and low power; and NB-IoT for fixed sensors and meters that send small payloads and need years of battery life. Each technology is chosen to match the specific data, power, and coverage profile of the device.
How does the 1NCE partnership help?
As a certified 1NCE integration partner, monoZ brings 1NCE's global IoT SIM platform — covering 173 countries and 30M connected products — directly into the monoZ stack. This means your deployment has global coverage from day one, without separate SIM procurement, local operator negotiations, or fragmented data management across regions.
Can Mesimo scale across markets?
Yes. The combination of Mesimo's MVNO partnerships and the 1NCE global platform means the same connectivity stack works from a pilot of ten devices to a fleet of hundreds of thousands — across markets, without renegotiating operator contracts or restructuring the data pipeline as volume grows.
