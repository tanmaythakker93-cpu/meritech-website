Overview

A tool built for verdict, not viewing.

Sigma-PA captures the protocol truth. Sigma-PA is where that ground truth becomes a decision someone can sign their name to — an actionable recommendation, a vendor acceptance, a regulatory submission, a regulatory roadmap.

It ingests Sigma-LA logs instantly, replays them at any speed, decodes at a protocol layer not available over-the-air, and exports to MS Excel, SQL, and standard formats. Our on-the-box reporting modules mean a report ready within minutes of upload — not a half-day of isolation behind every number.

One purpose.

Sigma-PA folds replay, decode, and reporting into a single workflow — designed by the same engineers who build Sigma-AQ and Sigma-LA. The tabs below show what it replays, what it exports, and why it reduces time-to-decision.

  • Ingests Sigma-LA formatted log files with full protocol conversion side-by-side
  • Replays sessions at configurable speed with 1, 2+ graph views
  • Reads synchronised protocol, app, call, and graph views simultaneously
  • Filters call timelines, timestamps, and applies specific search string lookups
  • Works and compares multiple operators side-by-side
  • Build and save custom report templates for repeat campaigns
  • Full L1–L4 protocol decode from Sigma-LA log files
  • Multi-RAT: 5G NR, LTE, WCDMA, GSM log analysis
  • Export to MS Excel, SQL, CSV, XML, KMZ, and standard industry formats
  • ETSI TS 102 250 and ITU-T E.804 QoS/QoE KPI calculation
  • On-the-box automated report generation on upload
  • Customisable KPI templates, thresholds, and report layouts
