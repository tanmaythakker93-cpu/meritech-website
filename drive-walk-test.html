Overview

Ground truth.
Not a prediction.

Drive and walk test data is the single source of truth that separates a network design from network reality. Propagation models are predictions. Drive test data is fact — georeferenced, timestamped, and captured at Layer 1 through Layer 4, on a real device moving through real conditions.

Using Sigma-LA, Sigma-AQ, and Sigma-Neo simultaneously, Meritech captures multi-device parallel data across multiple UEs, multiple technologies, and multiple network layers in a single field session — with post-processing through Sigma-PA and cloud analytics through Centra-SD.

Four disciplines. One field campaign.

What the field
campaign covers.

01
Network performance evaluation

Physical layer measurement, KPI capture, and data throughput analysis against acceptance thresholds. Coverage sampling at greater than 1 GPS fix per second gives the spatial resolution engineering decisions require — not an interpolated estimate.

02
Coverage & handover analysis

Cell coverage boundary measurement, handover success rate, and serving cell allocation. Detailed mapping of overlapping coverage areas, handover triggers, and potential coverage gaps on the routes that generate subscriber experience issues.

03
Interference & signal quality

In-band and out-of-band interference identification, signal quality mapping, and root cause analysis. Channel-level decode identifies whether quality issues are interference-driven, coverage-driven, or configuration-driven — before parameter changes are recommended.

04
Benchmarking & optimization

Structured benchmarking against competing operators on the same route with the same methodology. Baseline capture before optimization changes and verification drive after — closing the loop between parameter changes and measurable improvement.
