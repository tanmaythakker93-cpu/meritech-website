HS
Photo
Harish Sachdeva
Founder & Chief Executive Officer
“Our clients don’t experience wireless, IoT, and IT as separate industries. They experience one system that has to work.”

Harish founded Meritech in 2003 with a conviction that mobile network engineers deserved better tools — built by people who understood the protocol stack, not just the business case. Over two decades, that conviction has scaled into a 300-person organisation spanning nine offices across seven countries, covering wireless test & measurement, low-power IoT, edge AI, and enterprise IT services. Under his leadership Meritech launched the Sigma suite in 2010, entered the IoT space in 2019 with monoZ, acquired Eoxys (AI/ML) in 2020, and brought AZENQOS into the portfolio in 2023.
