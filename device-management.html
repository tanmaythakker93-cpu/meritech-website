What's included

Everything your fleet
needs, managed.

Provisioning & zero-touch onboarding
Registering, configuring, and activating devices within the network without manual intervention. Zero-touch provisioning ensures that each device is authenticated, assigned its policy profile, and communicating securely before an operator ever touches the dashboard — whether you're onboarding ten devices or ten million.
OTA firmware updates & maintenance
Delivering secure over-the-air firmware updates across the entire fleet — scheduled batch rollouts, staged canary releases, or emergency patches — with automatic rollback if a device fails to apply an update. Field visits become the exception, not the rule.
Device health monitoring & diagnostics
Continuous telemetry from every endpoint — battery level, signal strength, uptime, error rates, and custom application metrics. Alerts fire before thresholds are breached, and remote diagnostics let engineers investigate and resolve issues without dispatching field technicians.
Remote configuration & control
Pushing configuration changes, parameter updates, and operational commands to individual devices or entire device groups over the air. Adjust reporting intervals, recalibrate sensors, restart services, or change connectivity profiles — all without touching the physical device.
Security & access management
Enforcing mutual authentication, certificate rotation, encrypted communications, and policy-based access control across every device in the fleet. Security posture is visible per device and per group — rogue devices are isolated, compromised credentials rotated, and audit trails maintained for compliance.
Fleet analytics & reporting
Aggregated dashboards and exportable reports covering fleet health, firmware adoption, connectivity uptime, and device lifecycle status. Gives operations, engineering, and product teams a single source of truth for every device — past, present, and projected.

Questions to ask

Before you choose a
device management service.

How does zero-touch provisioning actually work?
Devices are pre-registered against the fleet before they leave the factory or distribution point. On first power-up the device authenticates itself, pulls its configuration, and joins the fleet automatically — no operator action required. Meritech handles the certificate provisioning, policy assignment, and platform integration as part of the onboarding service.
Can you manage heterogeneous device populations?
Yes. Meritech Device Management is built to handle mixed fleets — different hardware generations, connectivity technologies (LPWA, cellular, Wi-Fi), and firmware versions — within a single management platform. Device groups let operators apply policies and updates selectively without affecting the whole fleet.
How are OTA firmware updates rolled out safely?
Updates are staged — deployed first to a canary group, validated against health metrics, then rolled out to the full fleet on a schedule. Automatic rollback triggers if a device fails to boot or reports errors after an update, so a bad firmware push can't brick the production fleet.
What happens when a device goes offline?
The platform tracks last-seen timestamps and expected heartbeat intervals for every device. An alert fires as soon as a device misses its check-in — operators see the exact device, its last known state, and its location before deciding whether to investigate remotely or dispatch a technician.
How is device security enforced across the fleet?
Every device communicates over mutually authenticated, encrypted channels. Certificates are rotated on schedule and on demand; policy violations isolate the offending device automatically. A fleet-wide security posture dashboard gives operations and security teams a single audit trail for every device, every command, and every update ever pushed.
