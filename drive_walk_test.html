Drive & Walk Test

The network on paper.
Verified on the road.

Meritech delivers drive and walk test campaigns to assess mobile network and service performance. Define the routes, devices, services, KPIs and reporting requirements with our engineering team.

Discuss your project

Overview

Ground truth.
Not a prediction.

Collect timestamped measurements and available location data along agreed routes. Select supported radio, protocol and application-level tests, then analyse compatible logs and report results using the agreed methodology.

Four disciplines. One field campaign.

What the field
campaign covers.

01
Performance evaluation

Measure supported radio and service KPIs and assess the results against agreed thresholds and sampling rules.

02
Coverage and mobility

Investigate measured coverage, serving-cell behaviour and mobility events along the sampled routes.

03
Signal-quality investigation

Use available RF, protocol and performance evidence to investigate coverage and interference concerns. Specialist RF measurements are scoped with the appropriate equipment.

04
Benchmarking and validation

Compare networks using aligned devices, scripts and methods. Where included, repeat measurements to assess the effect of agreed changes.
