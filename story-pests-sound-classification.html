Pests Sound Classification Device

A German consumer goods company needed an R&D prototype that could classify pest sounds to compute the density of pests — no traps, no counting by hand. Meritech built the hardware, the embedded software, and an audio ML model trained on 1000+ reference samples, reporting into a cloud dashboard that plots pest density in 3D.

Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

Count pests by listening to them.

Testing pest-control products means knowing how many pests are present — and counting them manually is slow, inconsistent, and unpleasant. The client needed an R&D prototype that could classify pest sounds to compute the density of pests: hardware and embedded software on the device, an audio ML model built from a collection of 1000+ audio reference samples, and a cloud dashboard — speaking HTTP and MQTT — that displays density of pests vs time vs each device in a 3D graph.

Approach & Solution

TinyML at the edge. A 3D picture in the cloud.

01
Embedded hardware on the XENO+ ML SOM

The device's embedded hardware was designed around Meritech's own XENO+ ML system-on-module — a platform built precisely for running machine-learning workloads on small, low-power edge devices.

02
Device embedded software

Firmware handles the audio capture chain and device operation — listening continuously, feeding the classifier, and reporting results upstream over HTTP and MQTT.

03
TinyML model for audio sound classification

A TinyML model, trained on the 1000+ audio reference samples, classifies pest sounds on the device itself — no raw audio streaming to the cloud, just the classification that matters.

04
Cloud dashboard on a customizable codebase

The backend dashboard, built on a customizable codebase, aggregates every device's readings and renders pest density against time and device in a 3D graph — the full test field visible in one view.

Business Value

No more counting bugs by hand.

Manual density calculations eliminated — the device computes pest density automatically, replacing slow and error-prone human counting.
Improved accuracy for product roadmaps — more reliable density data gives R&D a firmer basis for refining pest-control product decisions.
Reduced testing timeframes — pest density is computed continuously by the devices, shortening the cycles of product efficacy testing.
Fleet-wide 3D visibility — density vs time vs device in one 3D graph shows how an infestation — and the product fighting it — evolves across every test location.
