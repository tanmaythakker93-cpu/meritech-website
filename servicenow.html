Questions to ask

Before you choose
a ServiceNow partner.

Are you a certified ServiceNow partner?
Certification is the baseline for quality implementation. Meritech is a certified ServiceNow Consulting & Implementation partner — the platform is configured by people trained and accredited to do it, not learned on your project.
Which modules do we actually need?
A good partner scopes to your needs, not the whole catalogue. Meritech starts by consulting on which modules map to your real workflows — ITSM, HRSD, GRC, or beyond — so licence spend follows value rather than the sales deck.
How do you use the AI capabilities?
AI-driven routing and prediction is where modern platform value sits. Meritech implements ServiceNow's AI capabilities as part of the workflow design — intelligent routing, resolution suggestions, and prediction — not as an afterthought bolted on later.
Will you get our CMDB right?
Workflows are only as good as the data model beneath them. Meritech treats the CMDB as foundational — mapping the relationships across your IT estate into a single source of truth before layering automated workflows on top of it.
Do you optimize after go-live?
Implementation is the start; optimization is where ROI compounds. Meritech stays engaged after go-live — tuning live workflows, expanding across IT, HR, and customer service, and growing the platform's value as the organisation's needs evolve.
