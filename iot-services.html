IoT Services

Five pillars of
end-to-end IoT.

IoT Consulting
Before a single device ships, Meritech's IoT Consulting defines the business decision the data must support — then works backwards to select the right hardware, connectivity, cloud platform, and analytics layer. Strategy & use-case definition, device & edge selection, connectivity architecture, and pilot-to-scale planning — designed as a system, not assembled from parts.
Device Management
Managing a fleet of connected devices demands more than simple onboarding. Meritech provides end-to-end device lifecycle management — secure provisioning, remote configuration, real-time health monitoring, over-the-air firmware updates, and decommissioning — at scale. Built on Centra IoT, our centralized cloud device management platform, and backed by 25 years of field-deployed hardware experience.
Connectivity Solutions
Connecting devices is only the starting point. Meritech designs connectivity architectures fitted to your deployment reality — power budget, geographic coverage, message volume, latency requirements, and cost per endpoint over the fleet's lifetime. LPWA, cellular, BLE, and hybrid options evaluated on evidence. Backed by Mesimo, our global cellular IoT connectivity platform with single-SIM worldwide coverage.
Cloud Software Development
Data flowing off a device is worthless without a cloud layer that processes, stores, and surfaces it as decisions. Meritech builds IoT cloud platforms on AWS IoT Core, Microsoft Azure IoT Hub, and open standards — ingestion pipelines, device shadow services, real-time stream processing with Apache Kafka and Spark, scalable storage on DynamoDB and equivalent services, and the ML inference layer that closes the loop from sensor to insight.
Operations Support & Maintenance
An IoT deployment doesn't end at go-live — it runs for years in the field, across environments that change. Meritech provides continuous operations support and maintenance services: 24/7 infrastructure monitoring, proactive incident response, firmware and security patch management, performance optimisation, and capacity planning. Our dedicated support teams are always ready to address issues promptly and keep your IoT operations running seamlessly.
