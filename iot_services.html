The Meritech approach

Hardware to insight,
end to end.

Most IoT projects stall because hardware, connectivity, cloud, and analytics are chosen by different teams with different priorities — leaving gaps at every handoff. Meritech's IoT Services close that gap: one practice designs and delivers the full stack, from sensor hardware through edge intelligence, connectivity architecture, cloud data processing, and the analytics layer that turns data into decisions.

More than 20 years of engineering experience.

IoT Services

Five services for end-to-end IoT

Each service has its own page. Choose one, or combine them across the life of a deployment.

01

IoT Consulting

Before a single device ships, Meritech defines the business decision the data must support — then works backwards to select the right hardware, connectivity, cloud platform, and analytics layer. Designed as a system, not assembled from parts.

View service 02

Connectivity Solutions

Connectivity architectures fitted to your deployment reality — power budget, coverage, message volume, latency, and cost per endpoint. LPWA, cellular, BLE, and hybrid options evaluated on evidence, backed by Mesimo connectivity.

View service 03

Device Management

End-to-end device lifecycle management at scale — secure provisioning, remote configuration, real-time health monitoring, over-the-air firmware updates, and decommissioning. Built on Centra IoT, our centralized cloud device management platform.

View service 04

Cloud Software Development

IoT cloud platforms covering ingestion pipelines, device shadow services, real-time stream processing, scalable storage, and the ML inference layer that closes the loop from sensor to insight.

View service 05

Operations Support & Maintenance

An IoT deployment runs for years after go-live. Continuous infrastructure monitoring, proactive incident response, firmware and security patch management, performance optimisation, and capacity planning keep it running seamlessly.

How we deliver

From first conversation
to running fleet.

01 · CONSULT
Define the outcome

Align on the business decision the data must support — then derive the measurements, architecture, and connectivity requirements that make it possible.

02 · DESIGN
Build the architecture

Select device, edge, connectivity, cloud, and analytics layers against real requirements — with total cost of ownership modelled over the fleet's lifetime.

03 · DEPLOY
Pilot, then scale

Run a pilot designed to test what production depends on — coverage, battery life, data quality — then execute a rollout plan that carries the design into production.

04 · OPERATE
Support the fleet

Continuous operations support, firmware and security patch management, performance optimisation, and capacity planning — for as long as the fleet runs.
