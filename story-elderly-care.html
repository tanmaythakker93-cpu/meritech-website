Activity Monitoring System for Elderly Care

There are an increasing number of elderly people in Japan — and many other countries — living entirely alone. The client wanted IoT sensors placed throughout their homes to track movements, identify behavioural anomalies, and raise an alarm in any emergency — silently, reliably, 24 hours a day.

Geography
Japan
Domain

IoT · Web-based Solution
Products Used

On this page
Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

Sense the pattern. Know before it becomes a crisis.

The client needed a system that could sense the movement of elderly patients, identify their activity patterns, and report anomalies — automatically. The solution required an IoT platform web application that collected patient data from sensors placed in multiple rooms, surfaced it through an intuitive dashboard, and compared different types of movement over time to detect when something was wrong before a person called for help — or couldn't.

Approach & Solution

Sensors in every room. Intelligence in the cloud.

01
Real-time health monitoring tool

A live data collection platform ingesting patient movement data continuously — not in batches — so the system reflects what's happening now, not what happened an hour ago.

02
Hardware sensors with embedded software

Custom-designed sensor hardware placed throughout the home, each tracking multiple parameters and transmitting data to the cloud for analysis — self-contained, low-maintenance, no intervention from the resident required.

03
Movement tracking to a central system

Each sensor reports room-level presence and movement data to a central system, building a continuous record of daily patterns — what time the kitchen is visited, how long the bedroom is occupied, whether the bathroom was used during the night.

04
Anomaly detection and preventive action triggers

When the system detects a deviation from established patterns — no movement in the kitchen at a usual mealtime, prolonged absence from the bedroom — it flags the anomaly and triggers a preventive action alert to family or a care authority.

Business Value

A quiet system doing the watching so no one has to.

Timely family and authority alerts — movement anomalies and emergencies surface before they become tragedies, giving caregivers enough time to act.
Medication and activity tracking — the system can monitor adherence to routines and flag behavioural changes that indicate health deterioration.
Huge social impact — a scalable answer to one of Japan's most pressing demographic challenges: elderly people living alone without a constant physical caregiver presence.
Behavioural pattern analysis — the dashboard gives families and medical teams a longitudinal view of how a person's daily patterns change month over month.
