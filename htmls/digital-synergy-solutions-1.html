Overview

The 'synergy' in Digital Synergy.

Over time, the IT landscape of most businesses evolves into a motley of disparate systems, applications, and technologies. This requires skilful integration without impacting the performance and the character of the applications. Our integration architects design the blueprints to unify divergent systems through APIs and protocols — aligning every digital solution with the business objectives it serves.

Questions to ask

Before you choose a development partner.

The right questions to ask any digital services vendor — including us.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?
A new product that can't talk to your current stack creates another silo.
Do you cover the full lifecycle?
Ideation to deployment with one team avoids costly hand-off gaps.
How do you handle UX and security?
High-UX and secure shouldn't be a trade-off — ask how both are built in.
Web, mobile, hybrid, or PWA — which and why?
The right platform choice should be reasoned from your users, not the vendor's habit.
What's your track record and longevity?
20+ years of evolving web experience is a different risk profile than a new shop.
Will the solution align with our business objectives?
Technology should serve outcomes — ask how the build maps to your goals.
