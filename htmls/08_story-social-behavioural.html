Objective

Assess the skill. Personalize the coaching. Share it safely.

The goal was a web-based platform enabling individuals and institutions to join and assess their social skills. Assessment couldn't be a solo, one-off questionnaire: the application had to support online assessment collaboration and data sharing with partners, then close the loop — giving every user access to sessions and coaching materials customized to their assessment results, so a score always leads somewhere.

Approach & Solution

A social platform where the data is personal — literally.

Scalable, robust social media platform

The platform was designed from the outset for significant user growth — individuals and institutions joining across Europe and Japan — without re-architecture as the user base expands.

High degree of data security for confidential results

Behavioural assessment results are among the most personal data a platform can hold. A key emphasis throughout was maintaining a high degree of data security appropriate to this confidential data environment.

Collaboration and sharing without losing control

The hard part wasn't locking the data down — it was enabling collaboration and partner sharing on top of that security, so users decide what is shared, with whom, while everything else stays private.

Business Value

A platform people actually want to use.

Positive customer feedback — users responded well to a platform that treats a sensitive subject, personal skill assessment, with both rigor and care.
Intuitive design attracting new users — the interface itself became a growth driver, lowering the barrier for individuals and institutions to join and start assessing.
Assessment that leads to action — customized sessions and coaching materials turn every result into a concrete next step, not just a number.
Trusted partner sharing — institutions collaborate on assessments and share data with partners inside a security model built for confidential information.
