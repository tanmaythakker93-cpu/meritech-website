Objective

A cold chain is only as strong as its least-watched truck.

Maintaining optimal storage conditions for vaccines is crucial to ensure their efficacy — a single unnoticed temperature excursion in transit can render an entire shipment useless. The client needed a system providing stringent, continuous monitoring of their refrigerated fleet: where every truck is, what condition its cargo is in, and the location and status of field personnel as they maintain equipment and respond to customer requests.

Approach & Solution

Track the truck. Watch the cargo. Keep it simple.

Cold chain monitoring for every vehicle

Trucks carrying vaccines and medical drugs are monitored continuously in transit, so the storage conditions the cargo depends on are tracked for the whole journey — not just checked at departure and arrival.

Real-time location and status tracking

The system tracks the location and status of vehicles and personnel in real time — whether maintaining network infrastructure equipment or responding to customer requests, dispatchers always know who is where, doing what.

Android application built for the field

Delivered as a native Android application in Java — running on the devices drivers and field personnel already carry, with no dedicated in-cab hardware to install and maintain.

Simplified UI designed for drivers, not desks

The interface was deliberately kept easy to use and simple — a driver mid-route or a technician at a job site gets what they need in a glance, without training sessions or manuals.

Business Value

Vaccines that arrive as effective as they left.

> 92% accuracy achieved — location and status tracking reliable enough to run daily fleet operations and cold chain compliance on.
Easy to use, simplified UI — adopted by drivers and field personnel without friction, because the interface demands nothing beyond the job at hand.
Cold chain integrity in transit — vaccine and medical drug shipments are monitored through the most vulnerable part of the chain: the road.
Full fleet visibility for dispatch — the location and status of every vehicle and every field worker, live, in one system.
>92%

Tracking accuracy achieved across the fleet — for cargo where "probably fine" is not an acceptable answer.
