Diagnostic System for Air Conditioners

A Japanese air conditioner and home appliances manufacturer wanted to add environmental sensors to their products and monitor them remotely — improving the living quality of end users and giving commercial customers a way to track emissions. The result: a cloud platform receiving 50+ parameters from every device, every ten minutes, across two continents.

Objective

Every unit reporting. Every parameter decoded. Both directions.

The client needed a system that could receive data from IoT devices deployed across two continents, then decode and store 50+ different parameters — CO2, TVOC, dirt level, and more — arriving from every device every 10 minutes. Beyond passive monitoring, the platform had to support self-diagnosis of the air conditioner itself and enable cloud-to-device downlink communication, so the system could talk back to the hardware, not just listen to it.

Approach & Solution

One cloud platform. Two continents. Fifty parameters at a time.

AWS-based software built for global deployment

The platform was developed on AWS from day one with multi-region deployment in mind, so devices in different continents report into the same system with the same reliability — no regional forks, no duplicated infrastructure to maintain.

LPWA protocol (MQTT) for device communication

Devices communicate over MQTT — a lightweight publish/subscribe protocol built for constrained IoT hardware. The same channel carries sensor uplink and cloud-to-device downlink, enabling remote commands and self-diagnosis triggers.

Decode-and-store pipeline for 50+ parameters

Every 10 minutes, each device transmits an encoded payload of 50+ environmental and diagnostic parameters. The pipeline decodes, validates, and stores every reading — building the historical dataset that powers diagnostics and big-data analysis.

Interactive user dashboard

Both end users and commercial customers see their data through an interactive dashboard — current air quality at a glance for households, emission trends and exportable evidence for businesses pursuing certifications.

Business Value

Cleaner air you can prove, on two continents.

Deployed across 2 continents — the platform runs in production globally, collecting 1.6 million data points per year from every single device.
Emission tracking for certifications — commercial users track their emissions with real sensor data and use it to pursue building certifications such as LEED and WELL.
Big data analysis of environmental patterns — years of decoded parameter history enable analysis of air quality trends across regions, seasons, and device populations.
Better living conditions for end users — households see and understand the air they breathe, with the AC unit itself diagnosing its own condition before performance degrades.
