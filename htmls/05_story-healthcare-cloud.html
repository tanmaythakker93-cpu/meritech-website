Next Generation Healthcare — Cloud System

A major Japanese hospital chain operated isolated clinics across multiple cities with no interconnectivity — sharing a patient's medical records between locations was slow, manual, and error-prone. Meritech built one comprehensive, centralized cloud system connecting every clinic in the country, without compromising the strict privacy rules that govern patient data.

Objective

One patient. One record. Every clinic in the country.

The goal was to create a comprehensive and centralized cloud system connecting multiple clinics all over the country. A patient seen in one city should be a known patient in every other — their history, prescriptions, and results available to the practitioner in front of them, instantly. And because the system stores personal medical information, security and regulatory compliance couldn't be a layer added at the end; they had to be the foundation.

Approach & Solution

Security-first architecture, workflow-driven design.

Architecture designed around compliance

The system architecture incorporates robust security features built to meet the compliance requirements of storing Personal Information — encryption, access control, and audit trails designed in from the first diagram, not bolted on before launch.

BPMN workflows with views configuration

Clinical and administrative processes were modelled as BPMN workflows, with configurable views per role — so a doctor, a nurse, and a billing administrator each see exactly the screens and data their role requires, and nothing more.

Extensive multi-environment validation

Sanity, functional, and regression testing conducted across multiple environments before every release — because in a system holding live patient records, a regression isn't an inconvenience, it's a clinical risk.

Business Value

Records that travel with the patient — lawfully.

Extreme ease of data sharing — patient records flow between clinics instantly, much to the delight of patients and practitioners who previously waited on manual transfers.
Strong compliance with government privacy laws — the platform meets the regulatory requirements for storing and handling Personal Information end to end.
One centralized cloud system — a single source of truth replaces a patchwork of isolated clinic databases, cutting duplication and reconciliation work.
Role-specific dashboards — every user, from clinician to administrator, gets a view configured for their responsibilities, keeping sensitive data on a need-to-see basis.
1 system

Every clinic in the chain, all over the country, connected through a single centralized cloud — where there used to be an isolated database in every city.
