What's included

Models that
flex to you.

Staff augmentation
Add skilled professionals to your existing team — scaling capacity up or down to match the work, under your direction. You keep the management and the context; Meritech provides the hands.
Self-managed teams
A complete, self-managed team that owns a deliverable end-to-end — you set the outcome, they run the delivery. An outcome to hold accountable, not a to-do list to manage.
Complete IT function outsourcing
Hand an entire IT function to Meritech to run — so your organisation focuses on its core business, not its IT operations. The function is owned, staffed, and delivered end-to-end.
Hiring as a Service (HaaS)
Meritech handles the sourcing and hiring of IT talent on your behalf — the right people join your own team, without the recruitment overhead. You build the team; Meritech does the finding.

Questions to ask

Before you choose
an outsourcing partner.

Which engagement model actually fits us?
Augmentation, teams, function outsourcing, and HaaS solve different problems. Meritech starts by matching the model to your need — how much you want to manage, how fast you need capacity, and how long the engagement should run — rather than defaulting to one model for everyone.
How fast can you provide capacity?
Speed-to-skill is the main reason to outsource at all. Meritech provides skilled professionals without the months-long hiring cycle — sourcing, vetting, and onboarding are the service, not your overhead.
Who manages the work in each model?
Be clear on what you manage versus what they own. With Meritech the line is explicit: augmented staff work under your direction, self-managed teams own the deliverable, and function outsourcing means Meritech owns the operation end-to-end.
Can we scale up and down?
Flexibility is the value — fixed commitments erode it. Meritech's models scale capacity up and down with demand, so you pay for the capacity the work actually needs rather than carrying headcount through the quiet quarters.
Can models combine or evolve?
Needs change — the engagement should be able to flex. Meritech's models combine and evolve: an augmented hire can grow into a self-managed team, and a project team can transition into an ongoing outsourced function as the relationship matures.
