Telco Network Site Management

The client built a cloud-native platform for network site management serving one of the largest U.S. telecom providers — and needed technical support in building and managing this BPMN-based platform. A comprehensive network rollout lifecycle solution, managing every site from planning till operations.

USA · Germany

Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

Every site. Every stage. One workflow.

A network rollout is thousands of sites, each moving through the same long journey — candidate selection, permits, construction, integration, live operations. The goal was a comprehensive network rollout lifecycle management solution for managing network sites right from planning till operations — one platform where every site's status, next step, and owner is explicit, powered by BPMN workflows instead of spreadsheets and email chains.

Approach & Solution

Business process in. Camunda workflow out.

01
Onsite–offshore delivery model

The engagement was delivered with a team split between the client's location and offshore — keeping direct client contact where it matters while scaling engineering capacity where it's efficient.

02
Business analysis into BPMN (Camunda) workflow models

Business analysts elicited the client's requirements and converted them into formal BPMN workflow models — turning how the rollout actually works into executable process definitions, not just documentation.

03
Configuration of Camunda workflow models

The modelled processes were configured and deployed on the Camunda engine, so site lifecycle stages, approvals, and handoffs execute automatically and visibly across the platform.

04
Validation across multiple environments

Every release was validated through sanity, functional, and regression testing in multiple environments before reaching production — protecting a platform that a major operator's rollout depends on daily.

Business Value

A partner that works the way the client works.

Flexible team size — engineering capacity scales up and down with the rollout's phases, without the client carrying a fixed headcount.
Working on customer time zone — the team operates on the client's clock across the USA and Germany, so collaboration happens live, not overnight.
Direct dealing with clients and customers — engineers and analysts communicate directly with the client and their end customer, cutting the relay losses of layered account management.
Full lifecycle coverage — one BPMN-driven platform carries every network site from planning through build to live operations.
