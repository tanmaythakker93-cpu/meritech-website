Questions to ask

Before you choose
a validation partner.

Are you genuinely independent of the build?
Objectivity is the whole point — a builder can't grade their own work. Meritech's validation team has no stake in the build, so the evaluation reflects what the software actually does, not what its authors hope it does.
What testing methodologies do you apply?
Rigour comes from method, not ad-hoc clicking. Meritech applies rigorous, repeatable testing methodologies — structured coverage that finds what casual exploration misses, run the same way every time so results can be trusted and compared.
Do you cover systems, processes, and software?
A comprehensive evaluation looks beyond just the code. Meritech validates systems, processes, and software together — because reliable software produced by an unreliable process won't stay reliable for long.
How do you measure accuracy and performance?
Ask for the criteria, not just a pass/fail. Meritech agrees the readiness criteria up front — what accuracy, reliability, and performance mean for this software — and measures the results against them explicitly, so the assessment is defensible.
What does the evaluation deliver?
A clear, defensible assessment — not a vague thumbs-up. Meritech reports what's ready and what isn't, with the evidence behind each finding, so the ship/no-ship decision rests on facts rather than optimism. Fresh eyes exist to surface the blind spots your own team shares.
