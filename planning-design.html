Overview

The strategic foundation
the field depends on.

Every antenna placement, every coverage boundary, every frequency assignment starts with a plan. Planning & Design is where network economics and field reality meet — where propagation models, site surveys, and traffic assumptions are translated into infrastructure that actually performs.

Working across technologies — 5G NR, LTE, WCDMA, and legacy — Meritech's planning engineers use industry-standard simulation tools alongside proprietary measurement data from field campaigns to produce plans that close the gap between intent and outcome.

Four stages. One network design.

Before one network
design.

01
Site selection & placement optimization

We determine the optimal antenna locations from both coverage and capacity perspectives. Topographical surveys, 3D city models, and propagation modelling combine to identify candidate sites that deliver the coverage boundary and interference profile the network requires.

02
Propagation modelling & predictive analysis

We implement comprehensive radio propagation modelling and predictive analysis of coverage, capacity, and quality. Industry-standard tools alongside field-calibrated data produce models that reflect real terrain, real clutter, and real build environments.

03
Capacity planning & load balancing

Network dimensioning and traffic modelling to ensure the planned infrastructure handles peak and sustained demand. Load distribution analysis identifies hot spots before they become congestion events — capacity built into the plan, not added as an afterthought.

04
Frequency planning & spectrum optimization

Interference-free frequency assignment across sites, cells, and technology layers. Spectrum efficiency, co-channel interference analysis, and optimization of reuse patterns — ensuring every MHz of allocated spectrum contributes to the performance the plan promises.
