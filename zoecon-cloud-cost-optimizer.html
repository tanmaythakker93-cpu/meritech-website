Does it cover the whole estate continuously?
Sampled, periodic review leaves savings on the table. zoECON™ analyses the full estate continuously — every workload, every provider, on an ongoing basis rather than a quarterly exercise.
How much can it realistically save?
Ask any tool to be specific. zoECON™ targets up to 60% off the cloud bill — strong cost-optimization recommendations aimed at waste, right-sizing, and inefficiency, prioritised by impact.
Does it check security while optimizing?
Cutting cost shouldn't quietly open a risk. zoECON™ validates every optimization against your security posture — cost and security assessed together, not as separate exercises that never meet.

Rules catch the obvious; AI finds the rest. The C2O AI Engine surfaces non-obvious savings that rule-based checks and manual review miss — patterns across usage, sizing, and spend that only analysis at scale reveals.

Cloud spend creeps back — ongoing beats one-time. zoECON™ optimizes continuously and works across the major cloud providers, so the bill stays down as the estate grows and changes, without provider blind spots.
