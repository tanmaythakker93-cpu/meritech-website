Common Questions

Before you talk to
an engineer.

The questions operators and vendors ask us most when they're evaluating the Sigma and Centra family.

Which technologies can a single capture rig measure at once?
All of them, in one pass. A single rig captures 5G NR SA and NSA, LTE and LTE-Advanced Pro, UMTS/HSPA+, GSM/GPRS/EDGE, and Wi-Fi 6E simultaneously, decoded L1 to L4 and time-aligned on one clock — so inter-technology handovers are measured directly rather than stitched together afterwards.
How many devices can you drive test simultaneously?
Sigma-LA captures up to 16 devices in lock-step from a single rig. For city- or country-scale campaigns, Sigma-AQ Boss orchestrates dozens of rigs in parallel, all feeding the same data model in Centra-SD.
Do the products work together, or is each one standalone?
Both. Every platform is usable on its own, but they share one data model — captures from Sigma-LA or Sigma-One flow straight into Sigma-PA for decode, Sigma-ML for analysis, and the Reporting Server for dashboards, with no format conversion or re-keying between stages.
Are the tools tied to a specific network vendor?
No. Capture, decode, and scoring are standards-based and vendor-agnostic — the same rig measures any operator on any RAN vendor's equipment, which is exactly what makes competitive benchmarking and multi-vendor acceptance campaigns possible.
Can Meritech run the campaigns, not just supply the tools?
Yes. Meritech wireless services teams deliver the full lifecycle — planning and design, drive and walk test, optimization, RAN tuning, and acceptance — using the same Sigma and Centra instruments, so the people running your campaign are the people who build the tools.
