Common Questions

Before you talk
to an engineer.

What's the difference between Xeno+ SOM and Xeno+ Nano ML?
The Xeno+ SOM is a general-purpose solderable module designed for intelligent and secure IoT applications — smart metering, asset tracking, industrial automation. The Xeno+ Nano ML adds a dedicated Syntiant NDP120 neural decision processor plus Wi-Fi + BLE 5.0 for always-on audio and sensor ML inference at the edge. If your application needs on-device ML — keyword spotting, anomaly detection, audio classification — the Nano ML is the right choice.
What neural processor does the Nano ML module use?
The Xeno+ Nano ML integrates the Syntiant NDP120 neural decision processor, which runs multiple audio and sensor ML models simultaneously at minimal power — below 1mW at 4kHz. It supports fully-connected networks up to 16 layers and is optimised for always-on audio classification, keyword spotting, and sensor anomaly detection workloads.
What do I need to build around the module?
You design the carrier board: connect audio microphones, I2C-based sensors, and a battery power circuit around the module. The ML core, neural processor, Wi-Fi + BLE radio, and connectivity certifications are all already inside. The Application Development Libs handle the framework layer — your team focuses on the product application, not the infrastructure beneath it.
How much faster is development with Xeno+?
Customers typically report up to 80% reduction in development time compared to integrating a standalone neural processor from discrete components. The pre-integrated application framework, FOTA update system, PKE-based device authentication, and pre-certified radio module eliminate months of bring-up work that would otherwise sit before application development could even begin.
Where can I find the full product range?
The full Xeno+ product range — datasheets, evaluation kits, hardware design files, and Application Development Libs — is available at exosy.com. Contact the Meritech team to discuss your application requirements, request samples, or get support selecting the right module for your design.
