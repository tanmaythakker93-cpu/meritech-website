Project stages and deliverables
Connected project stages

Planning assumptions guide field validation. Measurements inform analysis and, where included, optimisation recommendations and remeasurement.

Project deliverables — Agree the required logs, KPIs, findings and reports. Root-cause investigation and optimisation recommendations are included where specified in the scope.

Testing tools supported by engineering delivery

Measurement and analysis — Use compatible tool and log configurations to connect field evidence with investigation and reporting.

Sigma-LA

Windows drive test platform. Up to 4 UEs, 2 scanners and a GPS, with real-time L3 decode.
Sigma-AQ

Smartphone network and service testing, with remote control, log upload and template reporting when the workflow is configured.
Sigma-PA

Post-processing and problem analytics. L3 and PCAP analysis, KPI aggregation, Excel export and template reports.
Centra-SD

Central management of test devices: remote scheduling, automatic upload and map-based KPI views in the browser.
