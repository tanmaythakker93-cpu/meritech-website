Typeface

The whole site uses a single typeface: Noto Sans (Google Fonts; weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, and 700 italic), set once on body and re-declared on form controls and buttons, which don't inherit it. The only exception is the terminal widget on the Wireless page (.wl-term-head, .wl-term-num, .wl-term-label), which uses Consolas / SFMono / Menlo monospace at 14px and 26px.

Responsive behaviour

responsivestyle.css steps the large sizes down at breakpoints: 52/60px headings become 42px below ~1199px and 32px below ~575px. Hero headlines use clamp() so they scale continuously instead of jumping. Body (16px) and small text (14px) stay fixed at every width. The contact page carries its own breakpoint ladder for its headline (44/36/28px).

Scale ladder

The core ladder is 14 · 16 · 18 · 20 · 26 · 32 · 42 · 52 · 60 · 80, plus clamp() hero sizes. Distribution across both stylesheets: 14px ×326 · 16px ×213 · 18px ×98 · 20px ×68 · 26px ×37 · 32px ×64 · 42px ×37 · 52px ×99 · 60px ×20 · 80px ×2.

Documented exceptions (kept deliberately, everything else is on the ladder):
· 22px — .ab-hero-hq-line (bold tagline on the About hero, weight 700; a branded line, not a heading role) and .pf-headline's smallest responsive step on Sigma-AQ Boss (52 → 32 → 22)
· 44 / 36 / 28px — the contact page headline's own breakpoint steps (.ct-headline)
· 48px and 36 → 28px — News & Events CTA headline (.ne-cta-headline) and featured-article title (.ne-featured-title)
