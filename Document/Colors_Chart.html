Green tint system

Transparent washes of the primary green — rgba(0,162,79,α) — carry the brand through backgrounds without solid fills:

.03–.08 → card washes, icon plates, chip fills  ·  .12–.18 → badges, hero gradient glows  ·  .25–.30 → chip and badge borders. The blue equivalent rgba(35,63,137,α) follows the same steps for the IT division.

Text on dark — white opacity scale

.35 fine print
.45 muted
.55 body
.70 emphasis
.85 strong
#fff headings

On dark sections, text never uses gray hexes — always white at an opacity step. Body text on dark is rgba(255,255,255,.55) (23 uses, the most common step).

Notes

The page background site-wide is a subtle repeating texture image (images/common_imgs/background.png) set on body, not a flat color. Minor one-off greens on dark dashboards (#00C25F, #00C853, #06C860) appear in SVG illustration accents — treat them as illustration colors, not palette members. Near-duplicate neutrals remaining for a future pass: #F0EFEE / #F0EEEC / #F4F4F4 (hairlines) and #D8D5D5 / #D3D3D3 (strong borders). #E5E3E3, #E8E6E6 and #C8C5C5 were merged into #EEECEC on 13 Jul 2026.
