DevOps Consulting

A major US-based global IT and management consulting firm wanted to standardize its DevOps consulting offerings — documenting standard and non-standard scenarios and their solutions, so every engagement starts from proven practice instead of a blank page. Meritech built the pipelines, the security operations, and the playbooks behind it.

Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

Turn DevOps experience into DevOps doctrine.

The initiative involved implementing DevOps practices while documenting the processes as they were proven — identifying bottlenecks and their resolutions, and benchmarking for best practices along the way. The end goal wasn't one working pipeline; it was a standardized body of knowledge covering standard and non-standard scenarios, that the client's own consultants could take into any customer engagement.

Approach & Solution

Build it, prove it, write it down.

01
Practical pipeline approach, founded on a real process

Rather than starting from theory, the DevOps pipeline approach was established using the company's own Product Development DevOps Process as the foundation — practices already exercised by real product teams, generalized for consulting use.

02
Multiple pipeline implementation strategies, documented

Different customers arrive with different stacks, constraints, and maturity. Multiple pipeline implementation strategies were documented side by side — so consultants match the strategy to the customer instead of forcing one pattern everywhere.

03
Security operations built into the pipeline

Security wasn't a separate chapter: Penetration Testing and Vulnerability Testing using SAST/DAST tools were implemented and documented as part of the pipeline itself, making DevSecOps part of the standard offering.

Business Value

Every consultant sells the same, proven playbook.

Unified DevOps consulting offering — the client now delivers consistent DevOps consulting to its customers, whichever consultant and whichever geography the engagement lands in.
Comprehensive, standardized documentation — a reference body used during consulting engagements, covering standard and non-standard scenarios and their solutions.
Bottlenecks identified with resolutions — the pain points of DevOps adoption are catalogued alongside their fixes, so engagements skip the expensive rediscovery phase.
Benchmarked best practices — recommendations grounded in benchmarking, not opinion, including security operations with SAST/DAST built in.
