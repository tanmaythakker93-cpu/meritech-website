Sensor Watch for Patient Care

An Australian startup running a patient-care research program needed a BLE sensor watch wearable that could capture very specific parameters — including patient hand-movement data — on a 300mAh Li-Ion battery, in a compact watch form factor. Meritech built the watch, the firmware, and the ML-powered dashboard that turns movement into a recovery story.

Objective

Measure recovery from the wrist. Quietly, precisely, all day.

The client — an Australian startup — needed a BLE sensor watch wearable for patient care as part of a research program. The device had to capture very specific parameters, including patient hand-movement data, while operating on a 300mAh Li-Ion battery in a compact watch form factor. A research instrument, but one a patient wears like an ordinary watch — comfortable enough to forget, precise enough to publish.

Approach & Solution

A research instrument disguised as a wristwatch.

01
Hardware on a 33mm × 32mm PCB in watch form

The sensing, BLE radio, and battery management were engineered onto a 33 by 32 millimetre board designed in OrCAD Capture and Routing — a full data-capture device that fits inside a watch head.

02
Watch-like enclosure design

The device enclosure was designed as a watch-like casing — because patients in a care program should wear a watch, not a piece of lab equipment strapped to the wrist.

03
Embedded software for continuous capture

Firmware in Embedded C on FreeRTOS runs the sensor sampling and BLE connectivity within the power budget of a 300mAh cell — capturing the movement parameters the research protocol specifies, all day.

04
Backend cloud dashboard, built to specification

A cloud dashboard application developed per the project's specifications collects every patient's data and runs the analytics — including the ML layer that condenses raw movement into recovery insight.

Business Value

From raw wrist movement to a recovery summary.

Hand-movement data collected successfully — the customer gathers patient hand-movement data for patient-care analytics, exactly as the research program required.
ML-generated recovery summaries — the backend dashboard's machine-learning algorithm turns movement streams into a summary of each patient's recovery.
All-day wearability — a compact watch form factor on a 300mAh battery means patients actually wear the device, and the dataset stays continuous.
Research program enabled end to end — from custom hardware to cloud analytics, the startup got a complete instrument chain without building an engineering team.
ML insight

The dashboard's machine-learning algorithm reads weeks of hand-movement data and answers the question that matters: is this patient recovering?
