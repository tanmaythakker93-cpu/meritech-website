Nurse Operations Management System

A medical service provider needed a web-based solution and mobile application for nursing operations management — with leadership, including Nursing Directors, requiring intuitive 24/7 access to manage their nursing team effectively. Meritech delivered it in Flutter, and the client cut manpower needs by over 20%.

Geography
India
Domain
Healthcare
Project Type
Mobile App · Web-based Solution
Platforms
Android · iOS (Flutter)
On this page
Objective Approach & Solution Business Value Technologies Used
Objective

Every shift staffed. Every handover heard.

The goal was to develop Android and iOS applications enabling quick access to nurse assignments and schedules. Beyond viewing rosters, the system needed inter-nurse communication capabilities for discussing patient medical conditions across shifts — the handover conversations that keep care continuous — along with automated roster management covering shift scheduling, leave administration, and cross-departmental transfers.

Approach & Solution

One Flutter codebase. Both platforms. Always on.

01
Nursing operations analyzed first

The engagement began by analyzing the provider's nursing operations processes and workflows — shifts, leaves, transfers, handovers — so the system automates how nursing actually runs, not a generic HR template.

02
Flutter as the common app framework

Flutter was selected as the single development framework for both Android and iOS — one codebase to build and maintain, optimizing costs while keeping the experience consistent for every nurse regardless of device.

03
Secure cloud storage for round-the-clock access

Cloud-based data storage with robust security gives Nursing Directors and staff management access 24/7 — because nursing operations don't pause at office hours, and patient data demands protection.

Business Value

Fewer gaps in the roster. Fewer complaints at the desk.

Over 20% reduction in manpower — automated rostering and scheduling removed enough manual coordination work to measurably shrink the staffing overhead.
Enhanced shift management efficiency — nurses see assignments and schedules instantly on their own phones, and changes propagate without phone trees or notice boards.
Fewer patient complaints — improved nurse-to-nurse communication across shifts means patient conditions are handed over, not rediscovered.
Roster, leave and transfers automated — shift scheduling, leave administration, and cross-departmental transfers run through one system with 24/7 leadership visibility.
20%+
