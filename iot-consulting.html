The Meritech approach

Start with the decision,
not the device.

Most IoT projects fail before the first device ships — a sensor chosen before the question it answers, connectivity picked before the duty cycle is known, a pilot that was never designed to scale. Meritech's IoT Consulting works the other way: define the business decision the data must support, then design the device, connectivity, and cloud layers backwards from it.

Twenty-five years of building instruments, LPWA suites, and device-to-cloud platforms means the advice comes from teams who have shipped at industrial scale — not from a slide deck.

What's included

Advice across
every IoT layer.

IoT strategy & use-case definition
Defining the business decision the data must support — then working backwards to the measurements, update rates, and reliability the system actually needs. The use case sets the requirements; the requirements set the architecture, not the other way around.
Device & edge selection
Advising on sensors, modules, and edge intelligence — build, buy, or adapt — balanced against power budget, environment, certification, and unit economics. Backed by Meritech's own hardware practice: monoZ, Xeno+, and ruggedized field instruments.
Connectivity & network architecture
Choosing between LPWA, cellular, and hybrid connectivity on evidence: coverage in your actual deployment geography, message volume, latency tolerance, battery life, and cost per endpoint over the fleet's lifetime — not whatever the last vendor pitched.
Pilot-to-scale & operations planning
Designing the pilot so it proves what production needs proven — then planning device management, firmware updates, security, and total cost of ownership for the full fleet. The plan covers year five, not just the demo.

Questions to ask

Before you choose
an IoT consultant.

Have you actually shipped IoT at scale?
Advice is only as good as the deployments behind it. Meritech builds and operates its own device-to-cloud stack — monoZ, Xeno+, Centra IoT, Mesimo — deployed in elderly care, predictive maintenance, and smart infrastructure at industrial scale.
Do you start from the use case or the hardware?
Hardware-first projects collect data nobody uses. Meritech designs backwards from the decision — the business question sets the measurements, the measurements set the device and network, and nothing is specified that the use case doesn't justify.
Is the connectivity advice vendor-neutral?
The right network depends on your duty cycle, geography, and cost model — not on a reseller margin. Meritech weighs LPWA, cellular, and hybrid options on evidence: power budget, coverage in your actual territory, message volume, and cost per endpoint over the fleet's life.
Will the pilot survive production?
Most IoT initiatives die between PoC and production. Meritech designs the pilot as a rehearsal for scale — testing coverage, battery life, and data quality under production conditions, with the fleet-management and rollout plan written before the pilot starts.
What do we walk away with?
A complete, actionable architecture — not a slide deck. Every Meritech engagement ends in a plan you can execute: device and connectivity choices with the reasoning behind them, a costed pilot design, and a scale plan specific enough to hand to a delivery team on day one.
